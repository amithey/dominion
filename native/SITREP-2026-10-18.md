# The situation report: not missing what happens to you (2026-10-18)

**The request (player):** it is easy to miss things, an attack on you or something of yours hit; I want a system that gathers and summarises it. **Then (player):** the red stripes should exist only for what intelligence sees or warns of in advance; without intelligence you would not know you are being attacked.

## What you are told, and why

| | Source | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Intelligence warning** | Your agents in the enemy nation (network 60 or more, `espionage.INTEL_TIERS`: "Warning of attacks on you"), Satellite Reconnaissance, early-warning satellites, an airborne radar | **Red** card, an alarm tone, a ping on the minimap |
| **Report of damage** | A hit on your own building or unit | **Amber** card; the attacker is named only if you saw it or have intelligence on its nation (or only one nation is at war with you), otherwise "an unidentified force" |
| **Sighting** | Enemy forces you can see near your towns | A line in the report |

Without that cover a rival's **missile launch is not announced at all** (it used to be, to everyone, by name): you learn of it when it lands. An army on its way is announced only to a nation with agents inside the enemy; one you can see is a sighting.

## The report

- **Banner** under the top bar: at most three live alerts, intelligence warnings first; click one to look at the place.
- **Minimap** pings where you are hit or warned.
- **Situation report window (L)**: a summary of the last five minutes by kind (warnings, forces sighted, under attack, lost, destroyed, diplomacy and war, economy, completed, other), counts instead of repeats ("Lost: 3× Tank", forty hits on a barracks are one entry with its health), click a line to look there; a second tab keeps the full message log.
- **Log button** shows the number of unread warnings.
- When a **battle** goes quiet for 22 seconds a report says how it went ("you lost 4, the enemy lost 9").

Code: `scripts/sitrep.gd` (events, merging, detection), `scripts/sitrep_view.gd` (banner, window, badge), hooks in `world.damage`, `world.kill`, `world.destroy_building`, `hud.notice`, `ai.missile_strike`. Test: `tools/sitrep-check.gd`.
