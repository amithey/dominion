# Balance: weapons that were too strong for their price (2026-10-18)

**The report (player):** some weapons are over-powered and need balancing.

## Method

Every armed unit's *value for its price*: hit points x damage per second x the best damage multiplier it has (what it does to the class it was made to fight), divided by its price (money, with iron and oil at 3 and silicon and others at 5). A second, relative measure compares every unit with every other (hit points x damage per second x the real effectiveness against that target, over cost) and takes the average of the logarithm of the ratio. The tools are `tools/_balance-dump.gd` and `tools/_balance-matrix.gd` (development tools, not shipped).

Before: the ordinary units sat between 2 and 45 (a tank 21), the one-way weapons (they die on their strike) up to 125.

## What stood out, and the correction (`scripts/balance.gd`, applied once at the end of every nation's setup)

| Unit | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea drone | 380 damage, price 365; value 125 | 240 damage, price x1.3; value 60 | One hit nearly sank a destroyer (750 hp) for a third of its price. One no longer does. |
| APC, Bushmaster | 15 damage at 0.5 s (30 per second), price 370 | 10 damage (20 per second) | A troop carrier out-shot a tank (21 per second) and cost less. |
| Gunship | 36 damage, price 900; value 34 | 30 damage, price x1.15; value 25 | The best value in the game. |
| Interceptor drone | air multiplier 2.0, price 183; value 70 | 1.5, price x1.35; value 38 | A cheap kill on any jet. |
| Loitering munition, Harop | price 255 / 365 | x1.3 / x1.2 | One-way weapons at the top of the table. |
| Type 45 and AA vehicle | air multiplier 3.0 / 3.4 | 2.4 / 2.8 | The best-value warship was an air-defence destroyer. |
| Ballistic missile | 1700 damage, 900 money | 1450, 1100 | One missile flattened a block for less than a factory. |
| Tactical missile | 240 money | 290 | The cheapest way to hit a building. |

Left alone on purpose: the SAM launcher's value (34) is its design: it hurts only aircraft (multiplier 5.2) and reaches 115; the suicide squad (73) is a one-for-one charge.

## Test

`tools/balance-check.gd`: the corrections hold and apply once; no ordinary unit is above 45, no one-way weapon above 75, and the best ordinary unit is within five times the median.

This is a first pass from the numbers. Which weapons still feel strong in play is the better evidence; tell me and I will measure them.
