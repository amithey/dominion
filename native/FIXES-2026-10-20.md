# The report of 2026-10-20 (0.9.101)

| # | Report | What was wrong, and what it is now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Birds fly tail first; they should look better | The bird model's nose was +Z while `look_at` turns -Z to the target. They now fly nose first (`look_at(..., true)`), and were rebuilt: a streamlined body shaped to a head, a bill, a fanned tail (square for a gull, a wedge for a crow) and swept wings of two panels that bend as they beat; gulls glide between beats; crows are matte, not lacquered. |
| 2 | A carrier sent to attack sails straight at the target | A ship closes to 80% of its own guns' range: the carrier's guns reach 24 m, so it sailed up to its target while its jets were already striking. Ordered to attack, a carrier now holds at its air wing's reach (about 100 m, `war_tools.standoff`) and its jets strike from there; it keeps the target it was given; with its whole air wing lost it closes to its guns. |
| 3 | Troops inside a helicopter or ship are not noticeable | A green badge over each of your transports that carries anyone: a figure for each soldier and "7 ABOARD · 1 vehicle" (`unit_overlay._cargo`); a selected empty one says "EMPTY · 36 places". The selection panel heads the load in large letters ("7 ABOARD") and says how to load. |
| 4 | A missile fired "from a submarine" flies from the silo | A selected submarine's card offered every missile in storage; one that only a silo can launch then flew from the silo. The card now offers only what the selected ships can fire themselves (`hud._fill_launch`). |
| 5 | The AGM-88 cannot be used, and it is not clear what it does | It is an anti-radiation missile: it homes on enemy air-defence radar within 40 m of the aim point (triple damage, radar off for 30 seconds). For the United States it is **air-launched**, and only an aircraft already in the air counted: a jet waiting at its airfield now launches it too. When no launcher is ready the message says what launches it ("fired from an aircraft: a Jet, Bomber, Stealth Fighter or Raider... You have none ready") instead of "Needs a compatible air launch platform". |
| 6 | Some missiles and nuclear weapons cannot be used | A probe of every weapon with every launcher in play: all fire. What blocked them in play was the launcher: air-launched nuclear weapons (tactical nuclear bomb, nuclear cruise missile, bunker buster) needed an airborne jet (fixed as in 5), and a nuclear weapon of a nation with a Strategic Complex but no Missile Silo had no launcher at all: the Strategic Complex (and the Special Weapons Lab for its weapons) now launches what it holds. Each locked weapon says in plain words what it needs (`missiles.launchers_text`). |
| 7 | Dollar sanctions on Iran made no visible change | They worked (its income -30% for three minutes) but a rival's income is shown nowhere. Now the target's card in Diplomacy says "You: Dollar Sanctions: income -30%, 2:31 left" and lists the crises it is in; the sanction also enters its published GDP and inflation (crisis.gd), and the announcement says where to look. |
| 8 | Every text is unclear: "you get lost in the words" | `scripts/brief.gd`: a ROLE line and two or three POINTS instead of a paragraph, hand-written for 75 units ("Aircraft carrier: launches its jets · Attack: right-click an enemy: it stays ~100 m back, its jets strike · Jets land back aboard to rearm · No defence against submarines: escort it"); others shortened by rule (first sentence, two short ones); "United States only." and "Future programme / trials." become a small tag. Used on the build and training cards and in the selection panel; the full description is on hover. The controls are one line ("Right-click: move / attack · Ctrl + right-click: attack-move · Alt + right-click: bombard"). |

Also in 0.9.101: national crises and the statistics as they are published (CRISES-2026-10-20.md).
