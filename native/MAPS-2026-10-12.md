# More maps for the sea and the land (2026-10-12)

**The question (player):** make more maps to play on, both naval and land.

**What the game had:** 18 maps.
- 15 generated: from Small Isle to Pangaea and Ten Isles.
- 3 real: the Middle East, Europe, East Asia.

Few were built for a naval war, and none for a pure armour war on open ground.

## The six new maps (0.9.96)

| Map | Kind | Regions | What it is | How it plays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Strait** | sea | 6 | Two coasts face each other across a wide strait with an island chain down the middle. Only narrow causeways at the far ends join them. | Whoever holds the sea holds the crossing: landings, coastal batteries, carriers, helicopters carrying troops over. |
| **Ocean Atolls** | sea | 8 | Eight home islands on a ring in an open ocean scattered with atolls, tied by thin causeways. About 80% water. | A war of navies, carriers and air assault; the atolls are stepping stones. |
| **Great Steppe** | land | 8 | Open grassland almost to the map's edge, low hills, almost no water (97% land). | Armour, artillery, counter-battery radar, minefields; nowhere to hide. |
| **River Lands** | land | 6 | Two great winding rivers cut the land; the roads cross them at a few fords. | Hold the crossings; engineers and mines at the fords. |
| **The Gulf and Hormuz** | real | 8 | From Baghdad to the Arabian Sea and from Tehran to Sanaa. | Iran faces the Gulf states across the Strait of Hormuz; Saudi Arabia, Iraq and the Houthis start at their capitals. |
| **Red Sea and the Horn** | real | 9 | From Cairo to the Horn of Africa: the Red Sea, the Bab-el-Mandeb and the Gulf of Aden. | Egypt, Sudan (at Port Sudan, the army's seat of government), South Sudan, Ethiopia, the Houthis (Saada), Yemen's government (Aden) and Saudi Arabia start at their capitals. Asmara and Tabuk are open starts. The Houthis start at Saada, their northern heartland: Sanaa, the capital they hold, lies too close to Aden for two capitals on one map. |

**How they are built.**
- **Generated maps** (`map_generator.gd`). Each new map has its own shape:
  - every capital stands on dry land;
  - every capital is joined to the others by a land route, as on every map;
  - on the sea maps those routes are narrow causeways (`ROUTE_WIDTH`), so the sea still decides;
  - mountains are low on the Steppe and the Atolls.
- **Real maps** (`world_geography.gd`). They are new windows on the same world outline the other real maps use: a plain longitude–latitude projection, north up, with true start locations.
- **Previews.** Each map has a preview in the New Game picker (`ui/maps/*.png`, built by `tools/map-previews-build.gd`).

## Checks

- **`tools/more-maps-check.gd`:**
  - each map is in the picker with a preview, with the right number of regions, and every capital on dry land;
  - the geography is what it claims:
    - the strait down the middle;
    - the atolls mostly ocean;
    - the steppe open land;
    - the rivers cutting the land;
    - Hormuz, the Gulf, the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden water, with Arabia and Ethiopia land;
  - Iran, Saudi Arabia, Ethiopia and Egypt start at their capitals.
- **`tools/maps-check.gd`** (extended to the new maps), for every map:
  - capitals on land and reachable by land;
  - oil, iron and gold near each capital;
  - warships at sea.

## Judgement calls

- **Simplified outlines.** The real outlines are simplified to a degree or two. The Nile and the Ethiopian lakes are not drawn.
- **Sudan starts at Port Sudan.** The army's authorities sit there, and the game's Sudan is the army's state.
