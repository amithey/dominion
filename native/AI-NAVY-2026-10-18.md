# Rivals at sea, and the shape of their towns (2026-10-18)

## The sea (`scripts/ai_navy.gd`)

**The request (player):** the enemies should use the sea more.

What was wrong: a rival's attack waves took only land units, so its ships were built (when it built any) and left at the yard; a rival whose capital stood inland could not place a shipyard at all (it needs a coastal hex of its own land), so many never had one; and the land army's cap counted ships, so a nation with a full army never trained another.

Now a nation at war:

- **builds a shipyard**: at once when its army cannot walk to its enemy, and for any nation with a real navy (`war_tools.NAVY`) at war or established; if it holds no coast it **founds a town on the shore** first, and places the yard on its nearest coastal hex;
- **keeps a fleet** in proportion to its navy rating (ships have their own count; a short fleet is trained even with the land army full);
- **sorties**: a task force sails against the nearest enemy ships, or enemy coastal works (ports, shipyards, rigs, wharves, shore towns), stays about 160 seconds and returns;
- **defends** its coast: enemy ships near its buildings are attacked;
- **lands troops**: when there is no land route to the enemy (`path_between`), it raises landing ships, musters infantry and armour on the quay, sails with escorts to a beach near an enemy town, puts them ashore and the landed force attacks; the ship returns for another wave.

An unseen fleet is not announced (SITREP: you learn of it from radar and agents).

## Towns (`scripts/city_planner.gd`, `ai.gd`)

A rival's towns were strings of beads with gaps in the middle and far-off villages joined by long roads. Districts now score by how many of their own they touch (a gap in the middle of a town is worth filling first, a lone arm is penalised), the nation styles (grid, axial, linear) no longer stretch a town into a line, and a new town is founded every 11 buildings (not 7), 62 to 105 m out. Test: `tools/ai-city-check.gd` (gaps, lone arms, distance to the town centre after fourteen minutes).
