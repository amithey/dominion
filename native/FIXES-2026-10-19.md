# The sixteen points of 2026-10-19 (0.9.100)

| # | Report | What was wrong, and what it is now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ports and piers could be placed on coast you do not rule | The rule "harbours may go on unclaimed coast up to three hexes from your land" let a shipyard, port or wharf stand where you are not sovereign. Now they go only inside your territory; the message says to buy the coast first (Buy land at a town hall). |
| 2 | The birds looked bad | Two thin dark slivers. Now gulls (white, grey wings, dark tips) and crows (black): a body, a head and beak, a tail and wings of two parts that bend as they beat. |
| 3 | In winter the snow hid your territory and where you may build | The terrain shader painted the snow over the territory and building overlays. The snow now lies under them: borders and the green/red verdicts stay readable. |
| 4 | The UN should be more in the messages and more part of the game | New `un_agencies.gd` and a UN window tab **Agencies**: a calendar; the Secretary-General's briefing every few minutes; the General Assembly's general debate as each year begins; **IAEA** inspection rounds (a country with a weapons complex that is not a known weapon state is found in breach; the Council takes it up); **WFP and UNHCR** humanitarian appeals you can give to ($150 or $400, which earns goodwill) and that bring food when they are for you. Every item is a message that begins "UN" or the agency's name, filed by the situation report under diplomacy. See UN-AGENCIES-2026-10-19.md. |
| 5 | A unit blocked by another tries to push it | A unit on its way no longer shoves one that stands still: it steers round it (`tactics.crowd_push`, `still`). |
| 6 | Other nations' own research shows in your tree | Another nation's own programmes (a railgun, a foreign missile) are hidden from your research tree unless you have started them (`research.shown`). |
| 7 | A moving unit's badge trembles | The badge and health bar used the physics position, the model the interpolated one; they now follow the interpolated position. |
| 8 | Cancel, build over, or demolish a building | **Demolish** on the building card (click twice; half the cost back, 85% of a site's); placing a district or building over one of yours **replaces** it (the ghost turns amber, half the old one's cost back). The capital and towns cannot be demolished. |
| 9 | Rivals' towns are too spread out and illogical | The compact planner (0.9.99) held; a regression of mine (a string of "coastal" villages when it wanted a shipyard) was fixed: such a town is founded only when the army must cross water, and once. |
| 10 | Each spy should work on a job of its own | Intel > Operations: **Send** picks the agent for the job; the Agents tab has a **Send** button on each ready agent. Several jobs run at once. |
| 11, 16 | A shipyard that would not build warships; buildings near the sea or the edge that produce nothing | Production there works (probed on two maps for barracks, tank factories, airfields, helipads and shipyards). The cause that remains is **supply**: a building whose town is not linked to the capital by road waits, with a one-off message. A red **NO SUPPLY** sign now stands over such a building while its orders wait. |
| 12 | The Veterans panel's text was one letter a line | The row's second label had no width: now a minimum width, word wrap. |
| 13 | Fish could not be fished | A new **Fishing Platform** (at sea, on the fish school inside your waters; +4 food a second); the resource panel offers it. The coastal Wharf still works shoals within 60 m. |
| 14 | Natural gas on land, and winter without it | Every third oil field on land is now a gas field (an extractor works it). With no gas in winter: homes freeze: 0.4% of the people die each tick, happiness -10, factories at 75% speed, and a message; it lifts when gas returns. |
| 15 | You could build at sea anywhere by clicking a resource | The exclusive economic zone was seven rings of sea: now two. |

## Tests

`tools/fixes-1019-check.gd` (39 checks) covers 1, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 to 15 and the UN agencies; the existing `intel-100`, `espionage-teams-check` and `research` checks cover the rest of what moved.
